# 📘 CareerInPak — AdSense High-Value Content & Daily Publishing Guide (Phase 3)

Yeh guide **CareerInPak.com** par Google AdSense ke **"Low-value content"** error ko jad se khatam karne aur website ko ek authentic **Career & Educational Authority** banane ke liye master Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) hai.

Hum is guide ke mutabiq har roz ek original, high-value **Career Guide / Test Preparation Article** publish karenge taake Google reviewer ko clear signal mile ke website par genuine original value mojood hai.

---

## 🎯 1. Kamyabi Ka Asal Formula (Strategy Overview)

### Job Posts vs. Career Guides Ka Farq:
* **Job Posts (News Circulars):** Yeh official notifications aur akhbar ke ads hotay hain jo baqi 100 websites par bhi publish hotay hain. Google inhein "Duplicate / Thin" samajhta hai agar site par sirf yahi hon.
* **Career Guides (Pillar Content):** Yeh 1,000 se 1,500 words ke **Evergreen Original Articles** hotay hain jo student ko sikhate hain ke *test pass kaise karna hai*, *CV kaise banani hai*, aur *interview ki tayyari kaise karni hai*.
* **AdSense Approval Rule:** Jab kisi website par **30+ Active Job Posts** ke sath sath **6 se 10 Detailed Career Guides** mojood hotay hain, to Google usay educational resource maan kar **AdSense foran approve** kar deta hai.

## ✅ 6. AdSense Quality Pre-Publish Checklist (Har Article Ke Liye)

Har naya article publish karne se pehle yeh 10 checkpoints tick karein:

- [ ] **Word Count Check:** Article kam az kam **1,000+ words** ka ho (chota article na ho).
- [ ] **Pure Simple English:** Main article pure English mein ho, no Roman Urdu in paragraphs.
- [ ] **High-Contrast Text:** Text color `#111111` par set ho taake readability crystal clear ho.
- [ ] **Clean Headings:** H2 aur H3 headings structured hon, Red Banner styling (`#e53935`) ke sath.
- [ ] **No Keyword Stuffing:** Focus keyword 6 se 10 martaba naturally use ho (Density: 1.0% - 1.4%).
- [ ] **Original Practical Value:** Fake general lines ke bajaye real Pakistani books (e.g. Imtiaz Shahid, Dogar Publishers) aur real official portals ka zikr ho.
- [ ] **Internal Links:** Article ke andar CareerInPak ki doosri relevant job posts ke 2 se 4 links hon.
- [ ] **Clean Featured Image:** 1200x630 pixel landscape featured image (un-watermarked).
- [ ] **Category Correct:** Post ko `Career Guides` category assign ki gayi ho.
- [ ] **Cache Flush:** Publish ke baad `php artisan optimize:clear` run kiya gaya ho.
