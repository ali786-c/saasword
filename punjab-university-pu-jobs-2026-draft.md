## Short Introduction

The **University of the Punjab (PU Lahore)** has officially published **Advertisement No. 14/2026** (IPL-10801) announcing vacancies purely on a **Daily Wages / Contingent Basis** for an initial period of **89 days**. The recruitment is being carried out under the Policy Framework for Work Charged Employees / Daily Wagers / Contingent Paid Staff issued by the Government of the Punjab, S&GAD (Regulations Wing) and implemented by the University of the Punjab.

Applications are invited from eligible Pakistani nationals for the positions of **Driver (Contingent Paid Staff)**, **Mali (Female) (Contingent Paid Staff)**, and **Cook (Contingent Paid Staff)**. Candidates must submit their application online through the official portal (**http://111.68.103.6/nonteaching**) before **October 21, 2026 (4:00 PM)**, and dispatch signed hard copies along with attested academic and experience documents to the Office of the Registrar, Admin Block, Quaid-i-Azam Campus, Lahore.

## Who Can Apply

Pakistani male and female citizens fulfilling the prescribed qualification, practical experience, and physical fitness criteria are eligible to apply. The post of Driver requires Middle qualification (preferably Matric) and a valid commercial driving license (LTV + HTV). The post of Mali is strictly reserved for female applicants, with preference given to Primary pass candidates. Literate candidates with cooking experience can apply for the Cook position.

Candidates must note that these posts are purely temporary on an 89-day contingent basis, funded via Self-Supporting / Departmental Funds / Contingent Paid Staff Budget. The selected staff will have no right to claim regular appointment or regularization under any circumstances.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Driver (Contingent Paid Staff - 01 Post):** Middle pass, preferably with Matriculation. Must hold a valid commercial driving license (LTV + HTV) suitable for vans, cars, and university transit vehicles.
- **Mali - Female (Contingent Paid Staff - 01 Post):** Illiterate candidates are eligible; preference will be given to Primary pass individuals with practical gardening skills. Reserved for female candidates only.
- **Cook (Contingent Paid Staff - 01 Post):** Literate candidates with practical expertise in preparing meals and kitchen maintenance.
- **Terms of Engagement:** Purely on a Daily Wages / Contingent Basis for 89 days under the Punjab Government S&GAD framework dated 29.01.2021 (implemented vide No. 874/Est.ll dated 13.05.2024). This hiring shall neither confer any right of regular appointment nor can it be regularized under any circumstances. Candidates will have no right to file a court suit for continuation or regularization.

## Selection Process

1. Online application submission through the official portal: **http://111.68.103.6/nonteaching**.
2. Printing and signing the online form, attaching all attested credentials, testimonials, and photograph (1x1).
3. Dispatching the application packet via registered post or courier to: **Office of the Registrar (Administration Branch-II, Admin Block, Room No. 33), University of the Punjab Quaid-i-Azam Campus, Lahore**.
4. Administrative scrutiny of forms and verification of LTV/HTV licenses and educational background.
5. Practical driving test and formal interview for shortlisted candidates.
6. All communication during the selection procedure will be conducted through candidate email (dra2hr@pu.edu.pk) and contact numbers ((042) 99231108, 99232014).
