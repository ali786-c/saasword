# CareerInPak.com --- Deep SEO Audit & AI-Agent Implementation Brief

**Audit date:** 9 September 2026\
**Target:** https://careerinpak.com/\
**Purpose:** This document is written so a future AI/SEO agent can use
it as the baseline specification for improving CareerInPak.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Executive Summary

CareerInPak is an active Pakistan-focused jobs and scholarships website
with strong potential in the Government Jobs / PPSC / FPSC / NTS /
scholarships search space.

### Current estimated SEO score

**6.5/10 overall**

### Potential after implementation

**8.5--9/10**, assuming the site also has acceptable Core Web Vitals,
crawlability, backlink quality, and Google Search Console health.

### Strong points

-   Google is discovering and indexing individual job pages.
-   Fresh job content is published frequently.
-   Homepage clearly targets Government Jobs, PPSC, FPSC, NTS, remote
    jobs and scholarships.
-   Individual job pages contain useful structured information such as
    location, education, organization, deadline, vacancies and
    application instructions.
-   Official advertisement images are included.
-   About and Contact pages exist.
-   The site has a clear topical market: Pakistan jobs and scholarships.

### Main weaknesses

1.  Repetitive promotional/AI-style wording across job posts.
2.  Titles frequently use words such as "Massive", "Amazing",
    "Ultimate", "Exclusive", "Best", "Top", etc.
3.  Many older/expired job pages remain live and need an explicit expiry
    strategy.
4.  Tag pages are indexable and may create low-value taxonomy pages.
5.  Internal-link architecture can be much stronger.
6.  Author/E-E-A-T presentation is weak because posts commonly show
    "admin".
7.  JobPosting structured-data implementation needs direct verification
    in the source/Google Rich Results Test.
8.  Homepage taxonomy and topical hubs need stronger SEO architecture.
9.  Image filenames/alt text should be systematically optimized.
10. Several posts contain unnecessary filler instead of
    candidate-focused information.
11. Some pages contain mixed-language copy and templated phrases that
    should be normalized.
12. Search Console/server-level information was not available in this
    public audit, so crawl errors, Core Web Vitals, actual
    impressions/clicks, backlinks and indexed-vs-submitted URL counts
    remain to be verified.

# 4. Content Quality --- HIGH PRIORITY

## Current issue

Many job articles follow a highly templated style.

Examples of repeated language include: - "highly anticipated" - "golden
opportunity" - "brilliant pathway" - "incredible opportunity" - "massive
recruitment drive" - "prestigious career" - "phenomenal career
opportunity" - "world-class working environment" - "secure your
future" - "don't miss out"

Examples were found in pages such as: - FIA Jobs - Ministry of Defence
Jobs - Pakistan Rangers Punjab Jobs - KRL Jobs - Directorate of
Training/Health & Population jobs - CM Punjab Climate Internship

### SEO risk

The problem is NOT simply using AI.

The problem is publishing repetitive, low-value language that does not
help the applicant.

Future content should be **people-first** and information-dense.

### Required content philosophy

For every job post:

1.  Verify the official advertisement.
2.  Extract factual information.
3.  Explain the information clearly.
4.  Add only useful interpretation.
5.  Link to the official application source.
6.  State the deadline clearly.
7.  State whether the job is active or closed.
8.  Avoid generic motivational paragraphs.
9.  Avoid making unsupported claims.
10. Never invent benefits, permanence, salary, eligibility or selection
    details.

# 5. Recommended Standard Job-Post Template

Every job page should follow this structure.

## H1

`[Organization] Jobs 2026 – [Useful factual qualifier]`

Examples: - `PPSC Advertisement 9 2026 – 90+ Vacancies` -
`ECP Jobs 2026 – Senior Assistant & Sub Assistant` -
`KRL Jobs 2026 – Engineering and Medical Vacancies`

Avoid: - Amazing - Ultimate - Massive - Best - Exclusive - Top - Golden
Opportunity - Game Changer

Use those only if they are genuinely part of the source wording, not as
SEO decoration.

## Opening

2--4 factual sentences only.

## Job Summary

Use a compact table: - Organization - Department - Job type - Location -
Education - Total vacancies - Salary/pay scale - Last date - Application
method

## Vacant Positions

Clear table/list.

## Eligibility

Only requirements from the official advertisement.

## Age / Domicile / Quota

Include only when relevant.

## How to Apply

Step-by-step, factual.

## Official Apply Link

Link directly to the actual official application portal whenever
possible.

## Official Advertisement

Display the advertisement image.

## Source

Name/link the official source.

## Related Jobs

3--6 highly relevant internal links.

## FAQ

Only genuinely useful questions.

# 8. Taxonomy / Category / Tag Architecture

## Current observation

Tag pages are publicly accessible and indexable in search results.

Examples: - `/tag/fia-jobs/` - `/tag/security-jobs/`

This is not automatically bad, but low-value tag archives can create
index bloat.

## Recommended architecture

### Indexable primary categories

Keep a controlled set, such as:

-   Government Jobs
-   Punjab Jobs
-   Federal Jobs
-   PPSC Jobs
-   FPSC Jobs
-   NTS Jobs
-   Private Jobs
-   Remote Jobs
-   Scholarships
-   Internships

### High-value location hubs

Consider: - Jobs in Lahore - Jobs in Islamabad - Jobs in Rawalpindi -
Jobs in Karachi - Jobs in Faisalabad - Jobs in Multan - Jobs in
Sargodha - Jobs in Peshawar

Only create these when enough quality content exists.

### Tags

Do NOT create hundreds of thin tags.

Possible strategy: - noindex low-value tags; - index only strategic tags
with substantial unique content; - never create a tag for every post
name.

### Organization hubs

High-value long-term opportunity: - PPSC Jobs - FPSC Jobs - ECP Jobs -
WAPDA Jobs - Police Jobs - Army/Defence Jobs - Punjab Government Jobs

These should become curated topical hubs rather than simple archives.

# 10. Expired Job Strategy --- CRITICAL

This is one of the most important parts for a jobs website.

CareerInPak has many pages whose application deadlines have passed.

Examples observed: - PERA Jobs --- deadline 27 Aug 2026 - FIA Jobs ---
deadline 31 Aug 2026 - Ministry of Defence --- deadline 23 Aug 2026 -
Pakistan Rangers --- deadline 26 Aug 2026 - Islamabad Police ---
deadline 2 Sep 2026 - OGRA --- deadline 4 Sep 2026 - PEF --- deadline 7
Sep 2026

These pages should not simply remain looking like active jobs.

## Recommended approach

Do NOT automatically delete every expired job.

Instead:

### At the top of expired pages:

`Applications for this job are closed.`

### Then:

`Last date: 31 August 2026`

### Add:

`View latest [organization/category] jobs`

### Schema

Ensure `validThrough` is correct where JobPosting structured data is
used.

### If a job is permanently removed

Use the correct HTTP status: - 410 when intentionally gone and no
replacement; - 301 to a genuinely relevant replacement when appropriate.

Do NOT mass-redirect every expired job to the homepage.

# 11. JobPosting Structured Data --- CRITICAL TECHNICAL CHECK

This must be directly verified.

For real job pages, inspect JSON-LD and Google Rich Results Test.

Required/important properties include: - `@type: JobPosting` - `title` -
`description` - `datePosted` - `validThrough` - `hiringOrganization` -
`jobLocation` - `employmentType` - `baseSalary` where appropriate -
`identifier` where available

### Important

Never mark an expired job as active through incorrect structured data.

Never create JobPosting schema for: - category pages; - tag pages; -
generic informational pages; - scholarship pages; - pages without a
genuine job opportunity.

The schema must match visible page content.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 20. URL Strategy

Current URLs are generally readable, for example: - `/fia-jobs-2026/` -
`/krl-jobs-2026/` - `/ppsc-advertisement-9-2026-jobs/`

Keep URLs: - short; - lowercase; - hyphenated; - descriptive; - stable.

Avoid unnecessary changes because existing indexed URLs have accumulated
value.

Do NOT change URLs merely for cosmetic SEO.

If changing URLs: 1. 301 old → new. 2. Update internal links. 3. Update
sitemap. 4. Verify canonical. 5. Monitor Search Console.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 21. Duplicate Content / Programmatic Content Risk

A jobs site naturally has many pages with similar structures.

This is acceptable if each page has genuine unique information.

The danger is creating hundreds of pages where only: - organization
name; - location; - vacancy number; - a few words

change.

Future agent rule:

**Never generate a job page from a template unless the source
advertisement provides enough unique factual content.**

Unique content should include actual: - vacancies; - qualifications; -
quotas; - age; - deadlines; - application process; - organization; -
location; - official source.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 22. Content Length

Do not chase word count.

A 900-word job page can be better than a 2,000-word page if it contains
all important facts.

Recommended typical range:

### Simple job

600--1,000 useful words.

### Complex recruitment

1,000--1,800 useful words.

### Major PPSC/FPSC advertisement

1,500--2,500+ words if genuinely needed.

Never add filler merely to increase word count.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

targeting English search queries. - Urdu can be used for user-facing
application guidance where useful. - Avoid switching languages randomly
inside a paragraph. - Keep terminology consistent. - Use natural
Pakistani English rather than generic AI marketing language.
