## Short Introduction

The National Database & Registration Authority (NADRA) has announced new government jobs in Pakistan for the project-based role of Scanning Operator. Candidates with a Lahore domicile should check the required qualification, experience, age limit, and last date before applying online for these latest NADRA jobs in Lahore.

This is a temporary project based at the DRO Office in Lahore. The initial contract is for 3 months (90 days) and may be extended. Students, females, minorities, and differently-abled persons are encouraged to apply. The project involves archiving old records and scanning a minimum of 400 pages per day in morning, evening, or night shifts.

## Who Can Apply

Residents of Lahore holding a minimum Intermediate or equivalent qualification can apply. The maximum age limit is 25 years (which already includes the 5-year age relaxation). Candidates must be willing to work in morning, evening, or night shifts and handle electronic record archiving.

## Eligibility Criteria

- Candidates must hold a valid CNIC and Domicile of Lahore.
- Minimum educational qualification is Intermediate or Equivalent.
- Maximum age limit is 25 years (5 years age relaxation is already included).
- Candidates must be willing to work in any of the three shifts (Morning, Evening, Night) six days a week.
- Must be able to scan a minimum of 400 pages per day.
- Government or Semi-Government employees must provide a No Objection Certificate (NOC) at the time of the interview.

## Selection Process

Selected candidates will undergo an interview. No TA/DA will be provided for the interview. Candidates must bring original educational and professional documents. Selected individuals will be required to serve at the DRO Office in Lahore but may be posted elsewhere based on project requirements. Strict compliance with NADRA's Data and Information Security Policies is mandatory.
